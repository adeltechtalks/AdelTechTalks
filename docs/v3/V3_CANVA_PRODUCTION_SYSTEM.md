# V3_CANVA_PRODUCTION_SYSTEM

**Status:** proposal. **New in v3.1.**
**Scope:** how a design that exists in Figma becomes a post that goes out on
LinkedIn, Instagram or X, repeatedly, quickly, without the brand drifting and
without anything untrue being published.

---

## 1 · The division of labour, and why it is this way round

> **Figma is the source of truth. Canva is the publishing surface.**

Neither tool is good at the other's job.

Figma holds the design system — tokens, components, variants, the Arabic and RTL
variants, the misuse rules. It is where a change is *decided*, reviewed and
versioned. It is also slow and awkward for producing forty posts a month on a
phone between other things, which is the actual production reality.

Canva is fast, works on a phone, schedules to the platforms, and lets a
non-designer produce something on-brand in two minutes. It is also a tool that
will happily let anyone drag a heading, swap a font and add a gradient, and its
Arabic handling is not to be trusted without a rule around it.

So: **Canva templates are a build output, not a design.** They are produced from
the Figma masters, they carry locked layers around a small number of editable
fields, and when the Figma master changes, the Canva template is rebuilt rather
than patched.

```
Figma master  ──build──▶  Canva brand template  ──autofill──▶  scheduled post
   (decide)                  (produce)                            (publish)
      ▲                                                              │
      └──────────── a drift finding sends the change back here ──────┘
```

The one thing that never flows backwards: **a fix made in Canva is not a fix.**
It is a one-off that will be lost at the next rebuild. Anything that needs to be
true of every future post goes into Figma first.

## 2 · What Canva is not allowed to do

Stated as prohibitions because each of these is something Canva makes easy:

1. **No new colours.** The brand kit holds exactly the CS v3.0 palette. Nothing
   else is available in the picker for these templates.
2. **No new fonts.** Montserrat, Readex Pro, JetBrains Mono, and KO Ghorab
   uploaded as a brand font. No fifth face, no Canva default substitution.
3. **No gradients, ever.** Retired in v2.x; retired here.
4. **No stock photography of people.** Photography is real, and it comes from the
   asset library, not from Canva's search.
5. **No AI image generation** in any template. Not for backgrounds, not for
   "abstract tech", not for a placeholder.
6. **No editing outside the named fields.** Everything else on the canvas is
   locked. If a post needs a layout the template does not have, that is a new
   template, designed in Figma.
7. **No achievement cards.** Template 8 is generated by the site from a
   verification row (`V3_GAMIFICATION.md` §6.4) and does not exist in Canva. A
   share card that can be typed into is a share card that can claim anything.
8. **No numbers that are not real** — no follower counts, no "1000+ learners", no
   ratings, no rankings. The truth rules in `V3_CONTENT_OS.md` §3 apply to every
   asset produced here, and they are the reason a template has a *slot* for a
   figure only when a real figure exists to put in it.

## 3 · The ten templates

Every one exists as a Figma master, a Canva brand template, and a payload shape
the Content OS can autofill (`V3_CONTENT_OS.md` §4).

| # | template | sizes | editable fields | required real input | AR variant |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **AI News** | 1080×1350, 1200×630 | headline, source name, date, badge | a real source URL and date | yes |
| 2 | **Adel's Take** | 1080×1350 | the take (≤240 chars), topic, photo | the take, written by Adel | yes |
| 3 | **Prompt Card** | 1080×1350 | prompt title, the prompt, model tested on | a prompt that exists in the library | yes |
| 4 | **Six-slide Carousel** | 1080×1350 ×6 | per-slide heading + body, cover, end card | one idea per slide | yes |
| 5 | **Cheat Sheet** | 1080×1350, A4 | title, 5–8 steps, footer | verified reference content | yes |
| 6 | **Build Update** | 1080×1350 | project name, status, one line, screenshot | a real project and a real screenshot | yes |
| 7 | **Workflow Diagram** | 1080×1080, 1200×630 | 3–5 nodes, labels | a workflow actually run | yes |
| 8 | **Achievement Card** | 1200×630 | — | **generated by the site, not authored** | yes |
| 9 | **Reel Cover** | 1080×1920 | hook line, frame | a real video frame | yes |
| 10 | **Course / Resource Cover** | 1080×1350, 1200×630 | product name, outcome line, cover | a published product | yes |

**Every template ships with its Arabic variant as a separate page in the same
Canva template**, not as a "flip it in the app" instruction. Direction, alignment,
line breaks and the position of Latin islands are all different, and asking a
person to reconstruct that at post time is asking for it to be wrong.

## 4 · The bilingual rules Canva specifically gets wrong

This section exists because Canva's Arabic behaviour is the single largest source
of brand damage available in this workflow.

| rule | why | how it is enforced |
|---|---|---|
| **Arabic display text is KO Ghorab, ≥24px, weight 400, no letter-spacing** | the frozen typography rule; below 24px the face breaks down | the AR page's text styles are locked to the brand font at a minimum size; no free size field |
| **Arabic body and small UI text is Readex Pro** | Ghorab is a display face, and every small numeric or label element on a card is below its floor | separate locked style |
| **Latin terms stay Latin, inside an LTR island** | `AI`, `Prompt`, `Vibe Coding`, `Claude`, product names — the frozen non-translated list | each AR template has pre-built LTR text frames for these; they are not typed into the Arabic run |
| **Western digits everywhere** | consistency with the site, and with how the audience reads figures | locked; no Arabic-Indic numeral setting |
| **`الـ` never orphaned from its term** | it breaks the word | proofed at build; a line-break check is part of template QA |
| **Proof at the longest plausible string** | Arabic sets denser and overflow is the failure mode | every AR page is built and reviewed with its longest realistic content, not its shortest |
| **Never machine-translated** | the frozen authoring rule | the Arabic field in a Canva template is **empty by default**, never pre-filled with a translation of the English page. An empty AR field is a prompt to author; a translated one is a thing someone will ship |

**A post does not go out in Arabic because an Arabic template exists.** It goes out
in Arabic when Adel has written the Arabic. The template's job is to make that
authored copy look right, not to supply it.

## 5 · Autofill from the Content OS

Content OS phase 3 (`V3_CONTENT_OS.md` §6) targets this system rather than
generic "template rendering".

```
payload.json ──▶ template resolver ──▶ Canva autofill ──▶ draft design
                        │
                        └── refuses if payload.checks.blocking is non-empty
                        └── refuses if payload.authoring_required is true (AR)
                        └── refuses if a required real input is missing
```

Three properties this needs and does not get for free:

1. **The renderer asserts, it does not trust.** `adels_take: null`,
   `authoring_required: true` and a non-empty `checks.blocking` each stop the
   render. The safety of the pipeline is in those three fields, so the last stage
   re-checks them rather than assuming an earlier stage did.
2. **It produces a draft, never a scheduled post.** A human opens it, reads it,
   and publishes. There is no auto-publish path, in any phase.
3. **A missing asset collapses its slot; it never gets a placeholder.** The same
   rule the site follows. A template whose photo slot is empty renders the
   text-only variant, or it does not render.

## 6 · Keeping Figma and Canva in sync

The failure mode is silent: Figma moves, Canva does not, and three months of posts
are subtly off-brand before anyone notices.

**The contract:**

- Every Canva brand template carries a **version stamp** in a locked layer,
  matching the Figma master's version (`ATT-T03 v2.1`). It is small, in the
  bleed/margin, and it is not on the exported artwork — it is on the template.
- A Figma master change bumps the version and **the Canva template is rebuilt**.
  Patching the Canva copy to match is not permitted, because it leaves two
  designs that agree today.
- **A quarterly drift review**: export one asset from each of the nine authored
  templates, place them beside the Figma masters, and check colour, type, spacing
  and the Arabic variant. Findings go back to Figma.
- **Rebuild does not invalidate published posts.** Old posts stay as they were;
  the template governs what comes next.

**Ownership.** One person owns the Figma masters and the Canva rebuild. Anyone may
produce from a template; nobody edits one. This is a one-person product today, so
this reads as a formality — it is written down precisely because that is the
moment such rules are cheap to establish.

---

## 7 · What this system is deliberately not

- **Not a design tool for new formats.** New formats are designed in Figma. Canva
  produces; it does not decide.
- **Not a brand guardian.** Locked layers reduce accidents; they do not stop
  someone determined. The real guard is that there is one owner and a quarterly
  review.
- **Not connected to the website.** No Canva asset is fetched at build time and no
  page depends on one. If Canva disappeared tomorrow the site would be unaffected
  and the Figma masters would still be the design.
- **Not where achievement cards live.** Said twice on purpose.
