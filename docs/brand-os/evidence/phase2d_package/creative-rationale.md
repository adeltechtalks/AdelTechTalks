## Directions

### Direction A — Product hero

The strongest frame is the device itself, sharp and centred. With no creator asset the product carries the frame, and the panel names it rather than shouting a hook.  
Frame: **handling** at 170.58s · hook: GALAXY Z FOLD · text band: `bottom`

### Direction B — Curiosity — reveal withheld

The reveal beat with a short hook over the quiet band. Text asks the question the frame does not answer.  
Frame: **handling** at 170.58s · hook: WORTH IT? · text band: `bottom`

### Direction C — Bold concept — typography led

Typography is the idea; the frame supports it. Used when the product reads small at feed size.  
Frame: **seal_peel** at 91.97s · hook: FOLD IT · text band: `bottom`

## Recommended

**Direction B — Curiosity — reveal withheld** for every surface. The device is recognisable at feed size and the hook supplies the question the frame withholds. Direction A is the fallback where a platform discourages text; C is for a feed where the product reads too small.

## Rejected, and why

- **Creator only / creator + product / reaction** — *unavailable*. This script has no face detection, and the footage is hands-only: no creator frame exists to select. **Recommendation: shoot a dedicated thumbnail photograph** if a creator-led cover is wanted. Guessing one from video would be fabricating an asset.
- **A/B comparison, before/after** — one device, one state. No second subject exists.
- **Screen/UI dominant** — the boot screen appears only briefly and is too dim at feed size to carry a cover.

## Title / hook treatment

Hooks stay 2–5 words. For Galaxy Z Fold:

| Use | Hook | Why |
|---|---|---|
| recommended | **WORTH IT?** | a question the frame cannot answer |
| alternate | **FOLD IT** | verb-led, reads at 168 px |
| alternate | **FIRST FOLD** | positions the format, not the spec |

Titles are not hooks. A title may name the product; the cover hook must not repeat it.

## Brand treatment — fixed while composition varies

| Fixed | Varies |
|---|---|
| A-mark, same corner, same scale ratio | which beat becomes the hero |
| Signature Blue label, Spark Coral rule | whether there is a hook at all |
| hook in caps with a dark border | scrim depth, text band, type scale |
| text placed in the quietest band | the composition itself |

## Caveats

- **Montserrat is not installed here**; covers render in DejaVu Sans Bold. Structurally correct, typographically provisional.
- Source is 576×1024 WhatsApp-compressed, so a 1280×720 cover is upscaled. A camera original would be sharper.
- `reels-cover` and the other derived sizes keep the Phase 2B rule: no platform pixel size is invented as audited.
