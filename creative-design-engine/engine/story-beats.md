# Story beats

Ten beats. Each says what it needs, what it produces, and how it fails.

---

| Beat | The piece is… | Needs | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | opening a loop | one clear subject, short copy | the loop is closed in the same frame |
| **Reveal** | showing something not yet seen | a subject worth revealing | the thing revealed is ordinary |
| **Comparison** | putting two things side by side | two assets at matched scale and lighting | one side is favoured by framing |
| **Curiosity** | withholding an answer | a question the visual half-answers | the question is rhetorical or obvious |
| **Proof** | showing that a claim is true | evidence in the frame — a screen, a result, a before/after | the "proof" is a stock image |
| **Transformation** | showing a change of state | the same subject in two states, same framing | the two states are different subjects |
| **Explanation** | making something understood | a diagram, a sequence, or a labelled object | it becomes a lecture slide |
| **Joke** | landing a punchline | a subject with an incongruity | the joke needs a caption to work |
| **Demonstration** | showing use, not describing it | the thing in real use | it shows the product at rest |
| **CTA** | asking for an action | one action, stated once | it asks for three things |

## Choosing

**One primary beat.** If two feel equally true, the piece is probably two pieces.

Beats are not content types. A product review can be a reveal, a comparison, a proof or a joke depending on what it is trying to do — and the same footage supports all four with different treatment. This is the reason the engine does not map content type to layout.

## Beat and response

| Beat | Usually aiming for |
|---|---|
| Hook, Curiosity | curious |
| Reveal | surprised |
| Comparison, Proof | informed, challenged |
| Transformation | impressed |
| Joke | amused |
| Demonstration, Explanation | informed |
| CTA | — the response already happened |
