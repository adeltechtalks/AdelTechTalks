# Composition strategies

Sixteen strategies. **Options the engine selects from, never templates it fills.** The same content family with different assets must land on a different strategy — if it does not, this is a template library wearing a costume.

---

| Strategy | Hero | Minimum assets | Best beats | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Product Dominant** | product | 1 product frame | reveal, hook, proof | the product is small or poorly lit |
| **Face Dominant** | creator | 1 portrait | hook, curiosity, joke | the expression is neutral |
| **Face + Product** | creator + object | portrait + product | reveal, proof, demonstration | neither is large enough to lead |
| **Product A vs Product B** | two products | 2 matched frames | comparison | framing favours one side |
| **Product A + Creator + Product B** | creator adjudicating | 2 matched frames + portrait | comparison, challenge | the creator becomes a third product |
| **Before / After** | one subject, two states | 2 frames, same framing | transformation, proof | the states are different subjects |
| **Screen / UI Dominant** | the screen | 1 screenshot | demonstration, explanation | the UI is legible only at full size |
| **Product + Feature Callout** | product, one feature | 1 product frame | proof, explanation | more than two features are called out |
| **Hero Object + Hook** | one object | 1 frame | hook, curiosity | the object needs explaining |
| **Multi-object** | a set | 3+ frames or 1 group frame | reveal, explanation | it becomes a catalogue |
| **Transformation** | the change | 2 frames or 1 recomposed twice | transformation | the change is invisible at small size |
| **Editorial** | type and space | any, or none | explanation, CTA | it drifts into a presentation slide |
| **Minimal Premium** | one strong subject | 1 excellent frame | reveal | the subject is ordinary |
| **Playful / Social** | subject + wit | 1 frame | joke, curiosity | the joke needs a caption |
| **Explainer** | a diagram or sequence | any | explanation, proof | it holds more than one idea |
| **Device / Foldable** | the device or its screen shape | 1 device frame | reveal, transformation | no device imagery exists — say so |

## Selecting

1. **Assets filter.** Anything whose minimum the manifest cannot meet is out. This usually removes more than half.
2. **Beat ranks** what survives (`decision-matrix.md`).
3. **Response breaks ties** — see `decision-framework.md §5`.

## Structural rules

- **The hero takes the majority of visual attention.** Subjects may bleed off edges, overlap zones and sit behind type. Composed, not boxed.
- **No rigid 50/50 split unless the story genuinely is a comparison.** A split used as a default is the clearest tell of a template.
- **Never a panel drawn only to hold text.** Contrast comes from a global grade over the image; a local rectangle with a visible edge is a card, and cards read as templates.
- **Symmetry is a choice, not a default.** Most of these strategies are stronger asymmetric.

## The relationship to a brand's own archetypes

A brand profile may declare archetypes — named, pre-composed layouts. The engine treats them as **candidate instances of these strategies**, not as a lookup from content type to layout. An archetype is selected because the assets and beat point at its strategy, never because the content type has always used it.
