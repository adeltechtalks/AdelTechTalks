# /product-hero

**Put one object in front of everything else and let it carry the frame.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | Both |
| Best story beats | reveal, hook, proof |
| Best content types | product review, unboxing, first look, gear |
| Required assets | one product frame, lit, with enough resolution to bleed off an edge |
| Optional assets | environment for depth, a second frame for a detail inset |
| Max combinations | one supporting skill — usually `/light-sweep` or `/feature-callout` |

## When to use it
The object is genuinely interesting to look at and the piece is about the object itself. This is the default when a single strong product frame exists and no comparison is intended.

## When NOT to use it
The product is visually dull, poorly lit, or small in the source frame. Scaling a weak product up makes it look cheap rather than important. Use an editorial or type-led strategy instead and name the photography as a dependency.

## Composition logic
Subject occupies 40–70% of the canvas and bleeds off at least one edge. Ground is flat or a held-back environment. Hook sits on the calmest region, never in a panel. Nothing competes for the same visual weight.

## Motion logic
A slow push in, or the object settling into frame. One move, decelerating. No spin unless the object's form genuinely rewards rotation — that is `/orbit-product`.

## Brand OS rules
Ground, type and watermark from the profile. Flat colour only if the profile retires gradients. Subject scale rule comes from the profile's composition rules, not from this skill.

## Platform considerations
On surfaces with a square preview crop, the product must sit inside the centre-safe tile. On vertical the object is recomposed taller, not centre-cropped.

## Arabic / English considerations
Copy is short enough that direction barely matters. Keep established product names in their real Latin form inside RTL copy.

## Example
A camera filling the right 55% of a 16:9 frame, bleeding off the bottom, two-word hook on the dark left.

## Anti-patterns
A small product centred on a large empty ground · a drop shadow substituting for lighting · three features called out at once.
