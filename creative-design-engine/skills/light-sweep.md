# /light-sweep

**Pass a light across a subject to reveal form and lift it off the ground.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | Motion |
| Best story beats | reveal |
| Best content types | product reveals, premium moments, openers |
| Required assets | a subject with form worth revealing |
| Optional assets | a dark ground |
| Max combinations | this IS usually the supporting skill — do not add another |

## When to use it
A product reveal needs a moment of lift and the subject has surfaces that catch light meaningfully.

## When NOT to use it
The profile treats light effects as decoration, or the subject is flat. A sweep across a flat object is a shine on a rectangle.

## Composition logic
Light travels across; subject and camera stay still.

## Motion logic
One pass, one direction, and it leaves. A repeating sweep reads as a loading state.

## Brand OS rules
Only if the profile permits light effects; keep the intensity inside its limits. Never a lens flare, never a sparkle.

## Platform considerations
Lost at feed size — never rely on it to carry a thumbnail. It is a large-screen pleasure.

## Arabic / English considerations
Sweep direction may follow reading direction.

## Example
A single soft highlight crossing a matte product body once, then gone.

## Anti-patterns
Repeating sweeps · a sweep brighter than the subject · sweep plus glow plus shadow animation.
