# /cinematic-intro

**Open with a short, composed sequence that establishes tone before content begins.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | Motion |
| Best story beats | hook |
| Best content types | long-form openers, series intros, brand moments |
| Required assets | footage or stills with atmosphere |
| Optional assets | the brand mark, one line of type |
| Max combinations | one supporting skill — usually `/light-sweep` |

## When to use it
The piece is long enough to afford an opening. Earns its place on long-form, almost never on short.

## When NOT to use it
On short-form video. An intro on a thirty-second piece spends the hook on the brand instead of the viewer, which is the most reliable way to lose them.

## Composition logic
Two or three shots maximum, each held long enough to read. Ends where the content begins — no gap.

## Motion logic
Slow moves, decelerating. One cut rhythm. The brand moment is brief: a micro-moment, not a title sequence.

## Brand OS rules
Duration ceiling from the profile's motion charter. The mark appears once, small, and leaves.

## Platform considerations
Long-form only. On short vertical this skill is disqualified by the platform, not by taste.

## Arabic / English considerations
Any type follows the profile's language rules.

## Example
Two atmospheric shots and a half-second mark before the first spoken line.

## Anti-patterns
A five-second intro on a Short · a logo sting with sound design · an intro reused so often it becomes skippable.
