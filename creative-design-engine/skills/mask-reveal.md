# /mask-reveal

**Reveal content through a moving shape so the reveal itself has form.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | Motion |
| Best story beats | reveal, hook |
| Best content types | openers, transitions, brand moments |
| Required assets | the content being revealed |
| Optional assets | a shape drawn from the brand's own geometry |
| Max combinations | none — this is already a strong move |

## When to use it
A transition needs to feel authored rather than default, and there is a brand shape worth using as the mask.

## When NOT to use it
The shape is arbitrary. A mask in a random shape is a wipe with extra steps. Also not when the revealed content needs reading immediately.

## Composition logic
The mask travels; the content behind it stays still. Moving both reads as chaos.

## Motion logic
One pass, decelerating, and the mask exits the frame. Duration from the profile's motion charter.

## Brand OS rules
The mask shape should come from the profile's own geometry — its mark, its radius language — or be a plain rectangle. Never a novelty shape.

## Platform considerations
Works everywhere; costs a beat. On very short formats it may consume the whole hook.

## Arabic / English considerations
Direction of travel should follow reading direction where the profile specifies one.

## Example
A wide rectangle sweeping right-to-left in an RTL composition, exposing the subject beneath.

## Anti-patterns
Star or heart masks · two masks · a mask that stops mid-frame.
