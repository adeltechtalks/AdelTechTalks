# /object-track

**Follow a moving subject so the frame stays with it and the eye never loses the point.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Mode | Motion |
| Best story beats | demonstration, proof |
| Best content types | product in use, hands-on, unboxing, action |
| Required assets | footage with a clear moving subject |
| Optional assets | a mark that tracks with the subject |
| Max combinations | one supporting skill — usually `/sketch-overlay` locked to the subject |

## When to use it
The subject moves and staying with it is what makes the action legible.

## When NOT to use it
The subject barely moves. Tracking a near-static subject adds jitter and nothing else. Also avoid when the background carries information.

## Composition logic
Subject stays in a consistent screen position; the world moves around it.

## Motion logic
Smooth follow with slight lag — perfect tracking looks synthetic. Any attached annotation must track with the subject or it will appear to point at nothing.

## Brand OS rules
Any tracked mark uses the profile's annotation colour and rules.

## Platform considerations
Works everywhere. On vertical keep the subject off dead centre to leave caption room.

## Arabic / English considerations
Nothing language-specific.

## Example
A hand rotating a product, the frame holding the product centre while the desk slides behind.

## Anti-patterns
Tracking with visible jitter · a tracked label that drifts off the subject · tracking plus a push at once.
